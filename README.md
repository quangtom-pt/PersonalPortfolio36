# PLAY MY WORLD ✦ Bùi Quang Huy (Quang Tom) Portfolio

> *"A little green, a lot of ideas, data, and intuition."*

A responsive, single-page interactive personal portfolio website for **Bùi Quang Huy (Quang Tom)**, third-year International Business Economics student at Foreign Trade University (FTU), Hanoi.

---

## 🎮 Concept & Aesthetics
- **Theme**: Indie vintage + moss arcade + playful editorial + subtle Y2K + retro game UI.
- **Tone**: Young, energetic, observant, academically grounded in trade & logistics, deadpan calm, and quirky.
- **Rule**: Game wrapper with real content — recruiters can find essential facts within 10 seconds.
- **Palette**: Moss (`#3B4D3C`), Newsprint (`#F4F1EA`), Rust (`#C85A32`), Charcoal (`#222222`), Cream (`#FFF9F4`), with pastel stickers (Blush, Butter, Sky).

---

## 🗺️ Journey & Levels
1. **START SCREEN**: Hero banner with word cycling, player status, and 10s idle easter egg.
2. **CHALLENGE 01: Meet the Main Character**: Polaroid portrait, RPG attributes panel, flip personality tags, and a 10-second recruiter brief.
3. **CHALLENGE 02: Level Up My Brain (Education)**: Timeline map (Chuyên Hùng Vương ➔ FTU K63 IBE), knowledge cards, and academic badges.
4. **CHALLENGE 03: Unlock My Skill Set (Inventory)**: RPG item cards with rarity borders (Legendary Pencil, Calm Aura, Analyst's Lens, Customer Ears, etc.).
5. **CHALLENGE 04: Quest Log (Experience)**: C.A.S Logistics internship (Sales & Customer Services), IBE Community, and locked next quest slot.
6. **CHALLENGE 05: Boss Levels (Projects)**: Encounter placeholder card (`BOSS 01: Loading…`) with problem/move/result/tools matrix.
7. **CHALLENGE 06: Power-Ups & Side Quests**: 
   - Art & Random Archive (tilted Polaroids)
   - "Ask the Cards" Lenormand oracle widget (complete 36-card engine with 1-card & 3-card spread)
   - Sneaker sticker easter egg (`+50 XP` & hidden badge)
   - Jogging runner animation (press `J` or click track)
8. **FINAL LEVEL: Contact**: Game-item communication cards, quick dispatch form, 100% Level Cleared banner, and live achievements tracker.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No dependencies, build steps, or external image assets required!
